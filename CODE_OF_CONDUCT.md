# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to creating an open, welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community where everyone can learn, contribute, and collaborate.

We want people of different backgrounds, experience levels, and technical abilities to feel comfortable participating.

## Expected Behavior

We expect community members to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good intentions.
- Ask questions without fear of judgment.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Accept constructive criticism.
- Respect different technical opinions.
- Help newcomers.
- Focus discussions on ideas and implementations rather than individuals.
- Give credit to others for their contributions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or deliberate disruption.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Unwelcome sexual or personal attention.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Deliberately hostile or abusive behavior.
- Any other behavior that makes the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Technical Disagreements

Technical disagreement is expected.

We may disagree about:

- Architecture
- Programming languages
- Frameworks
- Databases
- Infrastructure
- Design patterns
- Implementation approaches

That's part of engineering.

Challenge the idea, explain your reasoning, and provide evidence where possible.

## Enforcement

Community maintainers may take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Possible actions include:

- A private warning
- Removal of inappropriate content
- Temporary restriction from community spaces
- Removal from the project

The response will depend on the severity and circumstances of the incident.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainers through the project's designated communication channel.

Reports will be treated respectfully and as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Final Principle

**Build software. Learn together. Respect each other.**
